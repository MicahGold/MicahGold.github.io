Adversarial Configurations for the ReCom Transition Function

Micah Gold · arXiv:2606.01333 (cs.DS, cs.DM), 2026

ReCom is the leading Markov chain Monte Carlo method for sampling redistricting plans, and it is widely used to detect partisan gerrymandering. I construct explicit 3-partitions of planar grid graphs from which ReCom needs an exponentially large expected number of steps to re-split, even when approximately balanced splits are allowed. So ReCom is not polynomial-time in the worst case.

STARE: Packing Spanning Trees for GPU-GPU Communication

Bill Qian, Micah Gold, Alex Schapiro · Yale, 2026

An open-source reconstruction of Microsoft Blink’s packed-spanning-tree collectives, built directly into NCCL’s graph-construction and algorithm-selection path for both intra- and inter-node AllReduce. It uses multiplicative-weights tree packing with greedy refinement. On H200 systems, STARE reaches up to 2.61× AllReduce bus bandwidth on fragmented topologies and 1.18× across two 8×H200 nodes. It stays at parity on symmetric NVSwitch hardware.

Trie and Trie Again: The Quest to GPU Parallelize MDTRIE

Micah Gold · Senior Thesis, Yale (Advisor: Anurag Khandelwal), 2026

MDTRIE is a succinct multi-dimensional index (from TRINITY, EuroSys ’24) that offers a memory-light alternative to FAISS-style retrieval for RAG, but its range search is sequential. This thesis builds the first GPU implementation. A co-designed split-stream per-layer trie layout and a warp-scan kernel reach a 70× speedup over CPU, with per-query latency nearly flat in index size. The thesis also identifies the representational limits that remain at embedding-scale dimensionality.

Bayesian Telephone: Memory Consolidation and Recall as Generative Processes

Dongyu Gong, Micah Gold · Yale

Does the hippocampus store raw sensory input, or generative reconstructions of it? We model the hippocampus as a modern Hopfield network and the entorhinal cortex as a variational autoencoder. We compare a raw-observation pathway with a Bayesian inverse-graphics pathway on rendered scenes, and find that both support reliable generative recall from noisy cues.
